# Problem Analysis / 问题分析

## Problem statement

> **People often waste time looking for small everyday items at home.**

典型对象包括 keys、earphones、chargers、power banks、remote controls 和 stationery。对象通常并没有离开房间，只是用户不知道它当前在哪里。

## Current 5 Whys

1. **Why can't people find their items?** Because they do not remember where they put them.
2. **Why don't they remember?** Because small objects are frequently moved and do not always have a fixed location.
3. **Why aren't objects always returned to the same place?** Because organizing everything manually takes time and effort.
4. **Why is manual organization difficult to maintain?** Because it must be repeated continuously, and people do not always remember or want to do it.
5. **Why does the problem keep coming back?** Because object locations keep changing, but there is no continuous mechanism to track and manage their locations and organization.

## Working Root Cause

> **Object locations constantly change, but they are not continuously tracked or managed.**

这是当前工作版本，不应被悄然改成“没有机器人”之类的技术结论。若后续研究显示它过宽或不够贴近用户，应在 [Consistency Check](Consistency.md) 中记录理由，再由团队确认。

## How Might We

> **How might we help people easily find and organize everyday objects at home?**

该问题保持开放，不预先规定必须使用机器人。机器人是后续比较多个方案后保留的当前方向。

## 需要验证的内容

- 哪一类用户最频繁、最愿意解决这个问题？
- “找东西”造成的实际时间、压力和不便有多大？
- 用户是否愿意改变收纳习惯，或接受一个会在家中移动并接触物品的设备？
- 哪些物品适合第一版支持？哪些物品不应支持？

以上均为 `Future validation`，当前没有调查数据或用户反馈可引用。
